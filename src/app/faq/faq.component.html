Fort Collins Top Dog is a fun fundraiser where dogs compete to raise money for their chosen charities, culminating in the crowning of the "Top Dog" who goes on an Odell beer can!

Dogs raise funds through RallyUp by getting donations from supporters. The Top 16 dogs with the most funds raised get to choose a charity they want to support and the Top 16 will compete in-the-fur at Odell's on September 20 th .
