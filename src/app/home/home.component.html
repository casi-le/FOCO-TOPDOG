In Person Competition at ODells Brewing Company

Fort Collins is known for its dogs. Thousands of cute, cuddly pooches call this city home. However, the age-old question remains… Who is the top dog in Fort Collins?! Who is the leader of the pack? The best in show? We have teamed up with Odell Brewing to officially answer this question, and support a good cause while doing it .

The winner gets their dog’s image on an in-production run can of Odell beer

The winning dog will have their photo be forever enshrined on a can of Odell beer that they are creating just for this event! We will work with the owner on either selecting an appropriate photo, or taking one specifically for the prize.

Every dollar you fundraise on your page will count as one vote for your dog.

The top 16 fundraisers and the most engaging social media star will get to move on to the in-person (in-pup) event at Odell Brewery in September.
