Fort Collins is known for its dogs. Thousands of cute, cuddly pooches call this city home. However, the age-old question remains… Who is the top dog in Fort Collins?! Who is the leader of the pack? The best in show? We have teamed up with Odell Brewing to officially answer this question, and support good causes while doing it .

It's Easy!

All you need to do is create a fun profile for your dog and raise money for a good cause. Make sure to use your dog's name in the profile instead of your own!

The winner gets their dog's image on an in-production run can of Odell beer

The winning dog will have their photo be forever enshrined on a can of Odell beer that they are creating just for this event! We will work with the owner on either selecting an appropriate photo, or taking one specifically for the prize.

Want to Enter Your Dog in Top Dog {{ftdYear}}? Here's How It Works:

  1. Sign up your pup starting July 1st and start fundraising for your favorite local charity (you'll choose from dropdown list).
  2. Fundraise through August 31st. Every dog who raises over $50 will each receive a prize package and a check from Top Dog made out to their chosen charity.

To enter, just create a profile on RallyUp using your pup's cutest photos and their name. This will be your fundraising page. Need help? Check out our guide.

Make your page fun, make it memorable, and show the world why your dog deserves to be Fort Collins' Top Dog!

So grab your leash, pick your cause, and get ready to compete for a good cause!

Pick Your Charity

Select from our pre-approved charities or submit a new charity for consideration. Approved charities will receive 100% of the funds raised by your profile. We even cover the credit card fees through our wonderful sponsors. Note - you must raise a minimum of $50 for a check to be issued to your charity of choice, otherwise funds stay with FoCo Top Dog, a 501(c)(3) public charity and will be used for community investment.
